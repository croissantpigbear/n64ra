N64RA Bridge for Mac
====================

RetroAchievements on a real Nintendo 64, through a SummerCart64.
Nothing to install: Python is included (Intel and Apple silicon).

1. Drag "N64RA Bridge" into your Applications folder.

2. First open: the app is not notarised, so macOS refuses it once.
   Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and press
   "Open Anyway" next to N64RA Bridge.
   Or in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/N64RA Bridge.app"

3. Plug the SC64's USB port into the Mac and press Start. If macOS asks
   to let N64RA Bridge find devices on your local network, say Allow
   (xeRAbora is reached over the network, even on the same Mac).

4. xeRAbora (the RetroAchievements client) starts with the bridge. The
   app finds one you already have (Downloads, Desktop, or one another
   app downloaded), or press Download. Sign in on its page once.

5. On the N64: put sc64menu.n64 from n64ra on the SD card, pick a game,
   press R for its options, set "RetroAchievements (USB)" to Enabled, and
   start it (an Expansion Pak is needed).

The window shows the game, its RetroAchievements hash, whether its
achievements are tracked, the frames sent, the USB link and the unlocks.
The first time a game starts, the bridge reads it back from the SC64 to
identify it (about 1 1/2 minutes for a 64 MB game).

If xeRAbora is already running, the app uses that one.
